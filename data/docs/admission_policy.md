# ABES College / Apex Institute - Admission Policy & Guidelines

## Eligibility Criteria
- **B.Tech Programs (Computer Science, IT, Electronics, Mechanical, Electrical):**
  - Minimum 60% aggregate marks in 10+2 (or equivalent) with Physics, Mathematics, and one optional subject (Chemistry / Computer Science / Information Technology / Biology).
  - Valid score in JEE Main or State Level University Entrance Examination (UPTAC / CUET).
- **Lateral Entry to B.Tech (2nd Year):**
  - 3-year Engineering Diploma or B.Sc. with Mathematics with minimum 60% aggregate marks.
- **Postgraduate Programs (M.Tech, MCA, MBA):**
  - Bachelor's degree in relevant discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories) and valid GATE/CAT/CUET-PG score.

## Application & Admission Process
1. **Online Application:** Prospective students must register online via the official university/college admission portal and submit personal and academic details.
2. **Document Verification:** Upload self-attested copies of 10th and 12th mark sheets, entrance exam score cards, transfer certificates, and identity proof (Aadhaar).
3. **Counseling & Merit List:** Merit lists are prepared based on entrance rank and academic scores. Candidates are allotted seats through centralized counseling rounds.
4. **Seat Acceptance & Fee Deposit:** Candidates allocated a seat must pay the initial seat acceptance fee within 7 working days to confirm their enrollment.

## Scholarships & Financial Aid
- Merit-based scholarships covering up to 50% tuition waiver for top 5% rankers in entrance exams.
- Need-based financial assistance available for economically weaker sections (EWS) as per state government guidelines.
